Havenmark Media 2026

Leader Briefing: Why Our Outreach Must Change — and How We Win (Eng & Chs behind)

1. Why Havenmark Media Must Change (Strategic Reality)

Listing portals now answer the questions buyers used to phone us to ask. Price
history, floor plans, school proximity and recent caveats are all one search
away. The old advantage — knowing things the client could not easily find — is
gone and it is not coming back.
门户网站已经回答了客户以前打电话问我们的问题，旧的信息优势已经消失。

What remains scarce is judgement: which of two similar units will be easier to
sell in three years, and why. That is what our content must demonstrate.

2. The Three Content Tiers

Tier 1 — Market Read. Monthly. One district, one question, backed by caveat data.
Published on the fifteenth of each month.
Tier 2 — Unit Study. Weekly. A single unit walked through on camera, with the
tradeoff stated plainly, including what is wrong with it.
Tier 3 — Answer Post. Daily. One real client question, answered in under 200
words, no call to action.

第三层每天发布，不带任何促销语句。
Tier 3 posts carry no promotional language at all. A daily post that sells is a
daily post nobody reads by week three.

3. AI Assistance Policy

3.1 AI may draft. AI may not publish.
AI可以起草，不可以直接发布。

3.2 Every AI-assisted post is reviewed by a named agent before publication, and
that agent's name is recorded against the post. The reviewer is accountable for
the claim, not the tool.

3.3 AI是不可以用来生成市场数据的。所有数字必须来自成交记录。
AI must never be used to generate market figures. Every number in a Havenmark
post comes from a lodged caveat or from our own transaction records, and the
source is stated.

3.4 A post that cites a figure without a source is taken down within one working
day of being flagged. This is a Major compliance finding, not an editorial note.

3.5 We do not generate images of units we have not visited. A rendered image of
a real address is a misrepresentation even when it is labelled.
我们不生成未曾实地到访单位的图片。

4. Response Time Standards

Portal enquiry — 20 minutes during working hours, 12 hours otherwise.
WhatsApp enquiry — 20 minutes during working hours.
Email enquiry — 4 hours during working hours.
门户网站询问必须在20分钟内回复。

An enquiry answered after two hours converts at roughly a third of the rate of
one answered inside twenty minutes. The standard exists because of that gap, not
because faster is politer.

5. What We Measure

We do not measure follower counts. Followers are not clients and the number is
easy to move without moving anything that matters.

We measure:
  5.1 Enquiries per published post, trailing 30 days.
  5.2 Median first-response time.
  5.3 Enquiry-to-viewing conversion.
  5.4 Viewing-to-offer conversion.

我们不统计粉丝数量。
Follower counts are not tracked.

6. Tone

Write the way you would speak to a client sitting across a table. No superlatives
we cannot evidence. No urgency we have not been given by an actual deadline.
不使用我们无法证明的最高级形容词。

The most persuasive thing a Havenmark post can do is state a drawback the client
would have found anyway, before they find it.
