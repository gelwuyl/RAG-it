Notes by project:

Fernhill Green — 528 units across nine blocks. Largest facilities deck in the EC portfolio. Buyers should be reminded that executive condominium eligibility conditions apply and that the minimum occupation period runs from the TOP date, not from the purchase date.

Copperleaf Rise — Earliest TOP in the EC portfolio at Q4 2027. 445 units. Lowest indicative PSF band of the four. Limited unit mix above three bedrooms.

Amberdale Court — Largest project by unit count at 636. Latest TOP at Q1 2029. The long runway suits buyers who are not under an occupation deadline and who prefer the lower entry that EC pricing offers.

Stonebrook Hill — 372 units, the smallest EC in the portfolio. Shares a district with Wrenfield Park but is a different tenure class and a different buyer profile; do not present the two as alternatives without explaining the eligibility difference.

Executive condominium commission is set by the developer and differs from both the standard residential sale rate and the private new launch rate. Confirm the rate in writing with the project marketing agency before the first viewing.

Eligibility summary — agents must not advise on eligibility beyond directing the client to the published conditions. Income ceiling, citizenship and existing property rules change without notice and an agent who states them from memory will eventually state them wrongly.
