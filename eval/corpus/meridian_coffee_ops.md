# Meridian Coffee — Operations Manual

## Stores and Hours

Meridian Coffee operates three stores: Riverside (opened 2019), Old Town
(opened 2021), and Harbor Point (opened 2023). All stores open at 6:30 AM
on weekdays and 7:30 AM on weekends. Closing time is 7:00 PM except
Harbor Point, which closes at 8:00 PM in summer.

## Menu Prices

The standard menu prices are: espresso 3.20, cappuccino 4.50, flat white
4.70, drip coffee 3.00, and cold brew 4.90. Oat milk adds 0.60 to any
drink. The loyalty card gives one free drip coffee after ten purchases.

## Opening Procedure

The first shift arrives 30 minutes before opening. The opening checklist
starts with the espresso machine: flush the group heads, check that the
boiler pressure reads between 1.0 and 1.2 bar, and calibrate the grinder
to a 27-second extraction for a double shot. Milk fridges must read
between 1 and 4 degrees Celsius; if above 5, call maintenance at
extension 204 and discard the stock.

## Cash Handling

Registers start the day with a 150 float. Drops above 200 go into the
drop safe every two hours. The closing shift counts drawers, completes
the reconciliation sheet, and locks cash in the office safe. Deposits go
to the bank on Monday and Thursday mornings only.

## Roasting

Meridian roasts in-house every Tuesday. The Riverside store houses the
Probat roaster. Light roasts end at first crack plus 30 seconds; the
house blend is roasted to second crack. Green beans are stored below
20 degrees Celsius and used within 90 days of arrival.
