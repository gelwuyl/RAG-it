1. Havenmark Method 恒安方法 (Must Read!)

Havenmark方法8个字: 制度化 数据化 人性化 长期化！
The Havenmark Method in four principles: Systematised, Data-led, Human, Long-term.

1.1 制度化 Systematised
每一个交易步骤都有清单，不靠记忆，不靠感觉。
Every transaction step has a checklist. We do not rely on memory or on instinct.
An agent who cannot produce the checklist for a stage is not ready to run that stage.

1.2 数据化 Data-led
每周更新成交数据，客户分级依据数据而非印象。
Transaction data is refreshed weekly. Client grading follows the data, never impression.

1.3 人性化 Human
客户的决定关系到一个家庭十年的生活，不是一张表格。
A client's decision shapes a decade of family life. It is not a form to be processed.

1.4 长期化 Long-term
我们做的是长期资产，不是一次性佣金。
We build long-term assets, not one-off commissions.

2. Client Grading 客户分级

2.1 把客户分四类: P1、P2、P3、观察。时间优先花在P1，P2每两周跟进一次，
P3交给系统自动培育，观察类不主动投入时间，等待信号。
Classify every client into four tiers — P1, P2, P3, and Watch. Spend time on P1
first, follow up P2 once every two weeks, let the system nurture P3
automatically, and invest no active time in Watch until a signal appears.

2.2 A P1 client meets three conditions at once: financing pre-checked, decision
maker identified, and a move-in or completion date within six months. Missing any
one of the three makes them P2, however enthusiastic they sound.

2.3 观察类客户不是坏客户，只是时机未到。
A Watch client is not a bad client. The timing is simply not yet right.

3. Efficiency Targets 效率目标

3.1 效率目标: Havenmark人均达行业平均2.5倍。
平均年成交目标: 全职 >18 单，兼职 >6 单。
Efficiency target: 2.5x the industry average per head.
Annual transaction targets: more than 18 cases per year for full-timers, more
than 6 for part-timers.

3.2 New agents are held to a ramp, not the full target: 4 cases in the first six
months, 9 cases in months seven to twelve. A new agent measured against 18 in
their first year will quit before the method has a chance to work.

3.3 单均佣金目标不低于 S$9,800。
Target average commission per case: not less than S$9,800. An agent hitting the
case count while falling below this figure is taking work that does not pay, and
their pipeline is reviewed rather than praised.

4. The Weekly Rhythm 周节奏

4.1 Monday — pipeline review, 30 minutes, P1 clients only.
4.2 Wednesday — viewing block. No internal meetings are scheduled on Wednesday.
4.3 Friday — data update. Every case in the system is moved to its true stage
before 6pm, whether or not it advanced.
4.4 周五数据不更新的顾问，下周不分配新线索。
An agent who does not update Friday data receives no new leads the following week.

5. Escalation 升级机制

5.1 Any case where the client has signed nothing after three viewings is escalated
to the team leader. Three viewings without a document is a qualification problem,
not a stock problem.

5.2 A dispute over co-broke split goes to the Practice Committee within two working
days. The committee's decision is final and is recorded in the case file.

5.3 佣金纠纷不在群里讨论。
Commission disputes are not discussed in group chats.

6. What Havenmark Does Not Do 我们不做的事

6.1 We do not quote a price we have not verified against a caveat lodged in the
last ninety days.
6.2 我们不承诺我们无法控制的批准结果。
We do not promise approval outcomes we do not control.
6.3 We do not take a listing we would not show to our own family.
