Havenmark Team Leader Standard 团队负责人标准

What a team leader owes their agents, and what they are measured on. Stage
requirements for Beacon and Keystone are set in the Pathway; this document is
about the job, not the ladder.
本文说明团队负责人的职责与考核，阶段要求以进阶体系文件为准。

1. SPAN 团队规模

1.1 A team leader carries no more than 8 agents. Beyond eight, the pipeline
review becomes a status report, and a status report is not a review.
一名负责人最多带8名顾问。

1.2 At most 3 of those agents may be at Ground stage at the same time. New
agents need supervised time, and three is the number that can have it.

1.3 A team leader with a full team who is asked to take a ninth agent should say
no, and the standard exists so that saying no is the supported answer rather
than the awkward one.

2. WHAT THE LEADER OWES 负责人的责任

2.1 Pipeline review, weekly, with the agenda set by the agent's own grading
rather than by the leader's curiosity.

2.2 One case file read in full per agent per month. Not a summary, not a
conversation about it — the file itself. Most findings are visible in the file
weeks before they become findings.
每月完整读一份案卷，不看摘要。

2.3 Attendance at a first viewing for every Ground-stage agent, and at any
viewing where the agent has asked for it, without asking why.

2.4 A decision within one working day on anything an agent is blocked on. A
leader who takes a week to answer has trained their team to guess.
顾问被卡住的事项，须在1个工作日内给出答复。

3. WHAT THE LEADER DOES NOT DO 不做的事

3.1 Take over a case. Sitting in is coaching; taking the client is not, and the
agent will not learn the part they were about to get wrong.

3.2 Allocate leads as reward or punishment. Allocation follows the published
rule, and a leader who varies it privately has replaced a system with a favour.
线索分配依既定规则，不作为奖惩手段。

3.3 Hear a compliance matter. Those go to the Practice Committee, including — and
especially — the ones the leader believes are trivial.

4. HOW A LEADER IS MEASURED 考核

4.1 Team retention over trailing twelve months, weighted highest. A team that
loses good agents has a leadership problem regardless of its volume.

4.2 The proportion of the team meeting their own stage requirements, not the
team's total transaction count. Total volume can be carried by one strong agent
and conceal four who are not developing.

4.3 Case file quality across the team, sampled quarterly by the practice
administrator.

4.4 Their own transactions count for no more than a quarter of the assessment. A
leader competing with their own team for stock is a structural problem that no
amount of personal production offsets.
个人成交在考核中占比不超过四分之一。

5. WHEN AN AGENT IS STRUGGLING 顾问表现不佳时

5.1 Name it in week two, not in month three. The conversation that feels
premature is the one that still has options in it.

5.2 Separate the two causes before choosing a response: an agent with no pipeline
needs activity, and an agent with a full pipeline and no closings needs
qualification. The remedies are opposite and applying the wrong one makes things
worse.
无管道与管道不成交是两个问题，处理方式相反。

5.3 A support plan runs 90 days, is written down, and states what will be
different and how it will be measured. An unwritten plan is a series of
disappointing conversations.

5.4 A leader may extend a plan once. A second extension is a decision nobody is
willing to make, and the agent is the person it costs most.
