SECTION 4 — LAND AND SUPPLY NOTE

The Jalan Rimau parcel closed at S$1,244 psf per plot ratio in September, below
both results reported earlier this year. Three of the four bidders were
mid-sized developers, which is itself the signal: the large houses sat out.
Agents should not read one quiet tender as a turn in the market.

SECTION 5 — CASE CLINIC (15 minutes)

Case E — a buyer asked to be released from an option after the cooling period
had passed. The agent explained the consequence in writing before the client
decided, and the file records the advice with its date. No finding.

Case F — an agent quoted a rental figure from a portal listing rather than from
a signed lease. The listing was an asking rent, not a transacted one. Escalated
the same day; the client was re-advised before the offer.
