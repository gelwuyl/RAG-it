Havenmark Paid Advertising Policy 付费广告政策

The outreach blueprint covers what agents publish organically and states that
paid advertising is out of its scope. This is that scope. Editorial standards
for claims and sourcing are set in the media briefing and apply here unchanged —
paying to distribute something does not lower the standard it has to meet.
本政策只涉及付费投放，内容标准以媒体简报为准。

1. BUDGET AND APPROVAL 预算与审批

1.1 An agent may spend up to S$500 per calendar month on portal boosts and
social promotion without approval, against their own budget.

1.2 Above S$500 and up to S$2,000, the team leader approves in writing before
the spend, not after it.

1.3 Above S$2,000, or any campaign naming a project rather than a listing, goes
to the partners. Project campaigns are coordinated with the developer's
marketing agency, and an uncoordinated one can breach the agency's own approval.
超过S$2,000或以项目名义投放的，须报合伙人。

1.4 Spend is recorded per listing. A campaign whose cost cannot be attributed to
a listing cannot be assessed, and will not be approved a second time.

2. WHAT MAY BE PROMOTED 可投放内容

2.1 A live listing with the owner's written marketing consent.
2.2 An agent's own profile.
2.3 A published Market Read, unchanged from the version that was reviewed.
2.4 一切投放内容必须与已审版本一致。
Promoted content must be identical to the reviewed version. An edit made inside
the advertising tool has not been reviewed.

3. WHAT MAY NOT BE PROMOTED 不可投放内容

3.1 A listing that is not yet live, including one where the agreement is signed
but the owner has not confirmed the marketing copy.
3.2 A price for a project, as distinct from an asking price for a specific unit.
3.3 Another agency's listing, in any form, including a screenshot.
3.4 A past transaction presented in a way that implies it is available.
不得投放已成交单位，使其看似仍可出售。

4. CLAIMS 陈述

4.1 Every factual claim in a paid advertisement carries a source in the same way
an organic post does, and the source must be nameable on request even when it
does not fit on the creative.

4.2 Superlatives are claims. "Best value in the district" is a statement about
every other unit in the district, and we do not have that data.
最高级形容词属于事实陈述，需有依据。

4.3 Rental yield, capital appreciation and rental demand may not be projected in
paid creative at all. They may be discussed with a client, with the assumptions
stated. A projection in an advertisement reaches people who never hear the
assumptions.

4.4 An advertisement carrying a figure without a source is taken down by the
agent who placed it, at their own cost, and the spend is not reimbursed.

5. TARGETING 投放对象

5.1 Targeting is by area, interest and language. Not by any protected
characteristic, and not by any proxy for one.
不得以受保护特征或其代理指标定向投放。

5.2 Lookalike audiences built from our own client lists are not permitted. That
is client data leaving the case system in a form we cannot retrieve, and the
client data policy governs it.

5.3 Retargeting a listing to people who viewed it is permitted for 30 days after
the listing goes live, and stops the day the listing does.

6. REVIEW 复核

6.1 Paid spend is reviewed monthly beside the enquiries it produced. Cost per
enquiry is the number, not impressions.
6.2 广告费用按月与询问数量一并复核。
6.3 A campaign producing enquiries that do not convert is a targeting problem,
not a budget problem, and more budget will reliably make it worse.
