Havenmark Pathway System 恒安进阶体系

Havenmark Creed (信条)

我们用长期资产观做地产业务，以制度化、数据化的方法经营，
磨练判断力，尊重每一个把家托付给我们的人。
We approach property as a long-term asset business, run by systematised and
data-led methods, sharpening judgement, and respecting everyone who trusts us
with a home.

PART A — THE FIVE STAGES 五个阶段

Stage 1 — Ground (地基) — Months 1 to 3
Objective: pass the Practice Test and close one supervised case.
Requirements:
  - Complete the Havenmark Foundation course (24 hours, in person).
  - Shadow 12 viewings before running one alone.
  - Pass the Practice Test at 80% or above. Two resits are allowed.
新人在通过实务测试前不得独立带看。
A new agent may not conduct a viewing alone before passing the Practice Test.

Stage 2 — Frame (架构) — Months 4 to 9
Objective: build a working pipeline of 20 graded clients.
Requirements:
  - 20 clients graded and current in the system.
  - 4 closed cases cumulative.
  - Run one Market Call segment for the team.
Exit is measured on pipeline health, not on income. An agent with four lucky
cases and no pipeline stays in Frame.

Stage 3 — Roof (封顶) — Months 10 to 18
Objective: independence.
Requirements:
  - 9 closed cases in the trailing twelve months.
  - Average commission per case at or above S$9,800.
  - Zero unresolved compliance findings.
封顶阶段的顾问可以自行定价，但仍需二人复核。
An agent at Roof stage may price independently, but a second reviewer is still
required.

Stage 4 — Beacon (灯塔) — Months 19 to 36
Objective: teach.
Requirements:
  - Mentor two agents through Ground.
  - 14 closed cases in the trailing twelve months.
  - Lead four Market Call sessions per year.
A Beacon agent's own numbers may dip by up to 15% while mentoring without
affecting standing. Teaching costs time and the standard accounts for it.

Stage 5 — Keystone (基石) — Month 37 onward
Objective: build the institution.
Requirements:
  - A team of six or more at Frame stage or above.
  - Team retention above 70% over trailing twenty-four months.
  - One system contribution per year, adopted firm-wide.
基石顾问的考核以团队留存率为主，个人成交为辅。
A Keystone agent is assessed primarily on team retention, and only secondarily
on personal transactions.

PART B — THE PRACTICE TEST 实务测试

The Practice Test has four sections, each weighted equally:
  Section 1 — Documentation. Which form, at which stage, signed by whom.
  Section 2 — Valuation. Reading caveats and adjusting for floor, facing and age.
  Section 3 — Financing. Loan-to-value limits and the timeline they impose.
  Section 4 — Conduct. What must be disclosed and when.

Pass mark is 80%. A candidate scoring below 50% in any single section fails the
whole test regardless of total, because an agent who is strong on three sections
and blind on the fourth will make the mistake the fourth section exists to
prevent.
任何单项低于50%即为不通过，无论总分多少。

PART C — CO-BROKE RULES 联合经纪规则

C.1 Default split is 50/50 between listing side and buyer side.
C.2 默认分成为五五分，除非在带看前以书面确认。
The default split is fifty-fifty unless agreed otherwise in writing BEFORE the
first viewing. A split renegotiated after an offer is made is not recognised.
C.3 A co-broke agreement must name both agencies, both agents, the property, and
the split. An agreement missing any of the four is not enforceable internally.
C.4 Introductions do not create a co-broke claim. Only a documented viewing or a
documented offer does.

PART D — THE CASE FILE 案卷

Every case carries a file, and the file is the record. Anything not in the file
did not happen.

Required contents, in order:
  D.1 Client grading sheet with the date of the last grade change.
  D.2 Viewing log — date, unit, attendees, outcome, in one line each.
  D.3 Every document issued, with its version.
  D.4 Financing status as at the most recent Friday.
  D.5 Any disclosure made to the client, with the date it was made.

案卷保存七年。
Case files are retained for seven years.

PART E — COMPLIANCE FINDINGS 合规问题

Findings are graded Minor, Major or Critical.

Minor — a missing date, an unversioned document. Corrected within five working
days, no standing impact.

Major — a document issued at the wrong stage, a grading not updated for more
than a month. Corrected within two working days, and the agent's next three
cases are reviewed.

Critical — an unverified price quoted to a client, an undisclosed conflict, a
co-broke split changed after an offer. The case is suspended immediately and the
Practice Committee convenes within one working day.
重大合规问题一经发现，案件立即暂停。

PART F — MARKET CALL 市场简报

Market Call runs monthly and is led by a Beacon or Keystone agent.

Fixed agenda, 45 minutes:
  F.1 Ten minutes — transaction data for the trailing month.
  F.2 Ten minutes — one project studied in depth.
  F.3 Fifteen minutes — case clinic, two live cases brought by agents.
  F.4 Ten minutes — one rule from this document, taught back by a Ground agent.

The final segment is deliberately given to the most junior person present.
Teaching a rule is how you find out whether you understand it.
最后一节由最新人主讲，这是检验理解的方式。

PART G — LEAVING 离开

An agent leaving Havenmark keeps their own client relationships. Case files
remain with the firm, because the file carries obligations to the client that
outlast the agent's employment.
顾问离开时带走客户关系，案卷留在公司。

Handover requires: every open case moved to its true stage, every P1 client
introduced in person to the receiving agent, and the Friday data updated on the
final working day.
