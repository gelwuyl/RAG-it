Havenmark Client Data Policy 客户资料政策

This is the policy the outreach blueprint defers to on client data handling. It
covers consent, storage, sharing and disposal of personal data. It does not
cover the case file, whose contents and retention period are set by the Pathway.
本政策不涉及案卷内容与保存期限，另见进阶体系文件。

1. WHAT WE COLLECT 收集范围

1.1 Collect what the transaction needs and nothing else. A grading sheet needs a
budget, a timeline and a decision maker. It does not need an employer, a salary
figure or a marital status, and an agent who records them has created an
obligation without creating any value.
只收集交易所需资料。

1.2 Financial documents are sighted, recorded as sighted with the date, and
returned. They are not retained by the agent. Where a lender or a lawyer needs
them, the client sends them to that party directly.
财务文件只作查验记录，不由顾问留存。

2. CONSENT 同意

2.1 Marketing consent is separate from transaction consent. A client who gives
us their number to arrange a viewing has not agreed to receive a monthly market
post, and treating the two as one is the most common complaint we receive.
看房联络方式不等于营销同意。

2.2 Marketing consent records are retained for 3 years from the date given, or
until withdrawn, whichever is earlier. The record states what was consented to,
on what date, and through which channel.

2.3 A withdrawal is actioned within 2 working days, across every list, and is
confirmed back to the client. Partial withdrawal — off one list, still on
another — is the failure this clause exists to prevent.
撤回同意须在2个工作日内在所有名单上执行并回复确认。

3. WHERE DATA MAY LIVE 存放位置

3.1 Client data lives in the case system. That is the record.

3.2 A messaging app may carry a conversation. It may not carry the only copy of
anything: any figure, date or instruction that matters is entered into the case
system the same working day.
聊天记录不得作为唯一记录。

3.3 Personal devices may access the case system. They may not store exports of
it. A spreadsheet of clients on a personal laptop is the single most common way
a firm loses control of its data, and it is usually created with good intentions
during a busy week.

3.4 Client data is never sent to a third party for analysis, enrichment or
scoring.
客户资料不得交由第三方分析或评分。

4. SHARING 分享

4.1 Within Havenmark, access follows the case. An agent who is not on the case
does not open the file.

4.2 Outside Havenmark, data goes only to the parties the transaction requires —
the lawyer, the lender, the developer's marketing agency — and only the fields
that party needs.

4.3 A referral partner receives the introduction, not the file.

5. DISPOSAL 处理

5.1 Where a client did not transact and consent has lapsed, their personal data
is deleted at the next quarterly disposal run. Grading history is retained in
aggregate form with the identifying fields removed.

5.2 Deletion means deletion, including from exports and from any list built for
a campaign. A record that survives in a spreadsheet was not deleted.
删除必须彻底，包括各类导出与名单。

6. WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG 事故处理

6.1 A suspected loss or exposure of client data is reported to the practice
administrator within 24 hours of being noticed, whatever the hour and whatever
the day.

6.2 Reporting is not a finding. Concealing is. An agent who reports promptly is
treated as having done the right thing, and the record says so.
及时上报不构成违规，隐瞒才是。

6.3 The client is told what happened, what data was involved, and what has been
done about it. They are told by a partner, not by the agent, so that the agent's
working relationship survives the conversation.
