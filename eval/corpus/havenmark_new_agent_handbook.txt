Havenmark New Agent Handbook — First Thirty Days 新人手册（前三十天）

This handbook is administrative. It tells a new agent what to arrange, in what
order, and who to ask. The standards a Ground-stage agent is held to — the
course, the shadowing requirement, the Practice Test and its pass mark — are set
out in the Pathway, and this handbook deliberately does not restate them. A
second copy of a threshold is the copy that goes stale.
本手册只讲流程与安排，标准以进阶体系文件为准。

DAY 1 — REGISTRATION 注册

Your licence registration is submitted by the practice administrator on your
first day. Submission takes about fifteen minutes; the register itself takes up
to 14 working days to reflect it. You may shadow during that window. You may not
be named on any document until registration is live.
注册期间可跟看，但不得出现在任何文件上。

Bring: identity document, academic certificate, the examination result slip, and
one passport photograph taken within the last six months. A photograph from a
holiday will be returned.

DAY 2 — SYSTEMS ACCESS 系统权限

Access is granted in three parts, and they arrive separately:
  - Case system, same day. This is where grading and the case file live.
  - Shared drive and calendar, same day.
  - Portal advertising account, after registration is live. This is the one that
    waits, and it is the one new agents assume is broken.

Your team leader approves the case system role. Ask for it in person; a request
sitting in a queue is not an approval.

DAY 3 — NAME CARDS AND PROFILE 名片与简介

Order name cards on day 3, not on day 1: they carry your registration number and
reprinting a box of five hundred is a waste of two weeks. Standard order is 300
cards, delivered in 5 working days.

Your public profile is written by you and reviewed by your team leader before it
is published. It states what you actually do. It does not state a specialisation
you have not yet transacted in.
简介只写事实，不写尚未成交的专长。

WEEK 1 — THE SCHEDULE 第一周

Monday — sit in on your team's pipeline review. You will not present.
Tuesday — case system walkthrough with the practice administrator, 90 minutes.
Wednesday — shadow viewings. Your first is with your team leader, not a peer.
Thursday — documentation session: which form exists, what it is for, and where
the current version lives. Versions matter more than wording.
Friday — you update your own data before 6pm, from your first week onward, even
when there is nothing in your pipeline to update. The habit is the point.

WEEK 2 TO WEEK 4 — BOOKING WHAT YOU NEED 预约安排

Foundation course dates are published quarterly. Book through the training
coordinator; seats are capped at 18 per intake and the intake nearest your start
date fills first.

Practice Test slots run on the first and third Thursday of each month. Book your
slot when you are ready, not when the calendar says you should be — a resit
costs a month, and the pass standard is set in the Pathway rather than here.

WHO TO ASK 找谁

Grading a client, pricing, anything a client will hear — your team leader.
Forms, versions, registration, access — the practice administrator.
A rule you think may have been broken — the Practice Committee, directly. You do
not need your team leader's permission to raise something, and raising it early
is treated as the correct behaviour rather than as an accusation.
发现可能的违规可直接联系实务委员会，无需经过团队负责人。

WHAT NOT TO DO IN YOUR FIRST MONTH 前三十天不要做的事

Do not quote a price, in any medium, to anyone, for any reason.
Do not answer a question about financing eligibility. Refer it, in writing.
不要在群里回答融资资格问题，请转介并留下书面记录。
Do not accept a client's document by photograph and treat it as sighted.
