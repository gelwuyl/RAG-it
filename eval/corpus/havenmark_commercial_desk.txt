Havenmark Commercial & Industrial Desk — Operating Standard
恒安商业与工业部 — 作业标准

Scope. This standard governs the commercial and industrial desk only. Residential
practice is governed by the Havenmark Method and the Pathway; where the two
differ, this document governs commercial cases and the Method governs residential
ones.
本文只适用于商业与工业部。住宅业务以恒安方法为准。

1. CLIENT GRADING ON THIS DESK 客户分级（本部专用）

1.1 The commercial desk does not use the residential grading scale. It grades on
its own three-point scale — C1, C2 and Hold — because a commercial requirement is
driven by a lease expiry or a board approval, not by a family's move-in date.
商业客户以 C1、C2、Hold 三级管理，不使用住宅分级。

1.2 A C1 requirement meets three conditions at once: a signed mandate or a board
paper, a stated occupation window, and a budget expressed as a rate per square
foot per month. A requirement missing any one of the three is C2, however urgent
the caller sounds.

1.3 C2 requirements are reviewed monthly, not fortnightly. A commercial cycle is
longer, and a two-week cadence produces calls with nothing new to say.

1.4 Hold covers requirements waiting on a lease expiry more than nine months
away. Diary them to the expiry month and invest no active time before it.
Hold 类需求等到期月再启动。

2. VOLUME AND FEE TARGETS 目标

2.1 A full-time commercial agent is measured on more than 9 completed
transactions per year. The residential case-count target does not apply here, and
neither does the residential average-commission figure: one industrial lease can
carry the fee of four residential rentals, so counting cases alone would reward
the wrong work.
商业顾问年成交目标为 9 单以上，住宅目标不适用。

2.2 Average fee per completed commercial case: not less than S$26,000. An agent
below that figure with the case count met is carrying small mandates that consume
the same process time as large ones, and their mandate mix is reviewed.

2.3 Mandate conversion is measured separately: at least 40% of signed mandates
should reach a transacted outcome within twelve months. A desk converting below
30% is taking mandates it cannot service.

3. THE DESK RHYTHM 部门节奏

3.1 Tuesday — mandate review, 45 minutes, C1 requirements only.
3.2 Thursday — inspection block. Commercial inspections are booked around the
sitting tenant, so they cannot be moved at short notice the way a vacant
residential viewing can.
3.3 周四为看房日，商业看房须提前与租户约定。
3.4 Month end — outgoings and service charge schedules refreshed for every live
mandate. A quote made on a stale service charge is a quote that will be
withdrawn.

4. ESCALATION ON THIS DESK 升级

4.1 A mandate open for more than 120 days with no offer goes to the desk head,
who decides whether to reprice it or release it.
4.2 Any request to quote a rate not supported by a comparable signed lease within
the last six months goes to the desk head immediately.
4.3 Tenancy novation, guarantor substitution and any assignment of lease go to
the Practice Committee before the client is advised.
4.4 商业租约的转让与担保变更须先经实务委员会。

5. WHAT THIS DESK DOES NOT DO 我们不做的事

5.1 We do not advise on fit-out cost. We name the party who can.
5.2 我们不对用途批准做承诺。
We do not promise change-of-use approval.
5.3 We do not act on both sides of an industrial sale. The conflict is too
concentrated where there are two parties and one comparable.
