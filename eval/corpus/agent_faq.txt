Havenmark Agent FAQ 顾问常见问题

This FAQ explains WHY the rules exist. It does not restate the rules themselves.
Where a number, a deadline or a threshold is involved, the Method and the
Pathway documents are the authority and this FAQ deliberately does not repeat
them — two copies of a number is one copy too many, and the second copy is
always the one that goes stale.

本文只解释原因，不重复具体数字。数字以方法与进阶体系文件为准。

Q. Why does client grading exist at all? Surely a good agent just knows who is
serious.

Every agent believes they can tell. The grading exists because that belief is
not measurable and does not survive a busy month. When forty conversations are
open, the ones that feel most promising are the ones that were most recently
flattering, and that is recency rather than judgement. Writing the grade down
converts a feeling into something a team leader can review.

分级的意义在于把感觉变成可以被复核的记录。

Q. Why is there a rule about who may run a viewing alone?

Because a viewing is where most of the irreversible mistakes are made. A price
mentioned casually cannot be unmentioned. A defect not raised becomes a defect
concealed. Shadowing is not ceremony; it is the cheapest place to watch someone
make those mistakes without a client paying for them.

Q. Why does the firm care about pipeline health rather than income?

Income measures last quarter. Pipeline measures next quarter. An agent can have
an excellent quarter through one large case and be in serious trouble, and the
income figure will not say so until it is too late to help. This is why stage
progression is assessed on pipeline and not on earnings.

Q. Why must data be updated on a fixed day rather than whenever convenient?

Because a pipeline that is updated whenever convenient is updated when it looks
good. A fixed day removes the choice, and the choice is the problem. It also
means every review is looking at data of the same age.

数据必须在固定日更新，否则更新的时机本身会带来偏差。

Q. Why is a co-broke split fixed before the first viewing rather than after?

Because after an offer exists, the split is negotiated by whoever has more
leverage rather than by whoever did more work. Fixing it beforehand is the only
moment when neither side knows who that will be.

Q. Why is a compliance finding graded rather than simply logged?

Because "a missing date" and "an unverified price quoted to a client" are not
the same event and must not produce the same response. Grading is what lets the
serious ones interrupt the work while the trivial ones are simply corrected.

Q. Why does the case file matter if the agent remembers the case?

Because the obligation belongs to the client, not to the agent, and it outlasts
the agent's employment. A file that exists only in someone's memory disappears
when they change firms, and the client's rights do not.

案卷是对客户的责任，不是对顾问的记录。

Q. Why does the most junior person teach the last segment of Market Call?

Because explaining a rule is the only reliable test of whether it is understood.
Someone who has read a rule can repeat it. Someone who can teach it has to
reconstruct why it exists, and that is where the gaps show.

Q. Why does the firm refuse to quote a price it has not verified?

Because an unverified price becomes the client's expectation within about a
minute, and every conversation afterwards is spent walking it back. It is
cheaper to say "I will check" than to spend three weeks correcting a number.

Q. Why is response time treated as a standard rather than as good manners?

Because the conversion difference is large enough to be a business rule rather
than a courtesy. The specific figures are in the media briefing.

Q. Why does the firm not track follower counts?

Because the number can be moved without moving anything that matters, and any
metric that can be improved without improving the business will eventually be
improved instead of the business.

Q. Why is AI allowed to draft but not to publish?

Because accountability cannot be delegated to a tool. A claim in a published
post is made by the firm, and somebody has to be answerable for it by name. The
tool cannot be called to a Practice Committee.

责任无法交给工具承担。
