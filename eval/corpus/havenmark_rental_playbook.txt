Havenmark Rental Playbook 租赁作业手册

Practice notes for rental cases. Which document is issued at which stage, and
the commission and timeline tables, are in the transaction checklists — this
playbook does not repeat them. What follows is the judgement around them.
本手册只讲操作判断，文件与时限以交易清单为准。

1. VIEWING A TENANTED UNIT 有租客的单位

1.1 A sitting tenant is given 24 hours' notice of a viewing, in writing, even
where the tenancy agreement permits shorter. The tenant controls whether the
viewing goes well, and notice is what buys their cooperation.
有租客单位看房须提前24小时书面通知。

1.2 Viewings are grouped. Four separate viewings in a week will end the tenant's
patience, and a hostile handover costs more than a slow letting.

1.3 Do not photograph a tenant's belongings. Marketing photographs of an
occupied unit are taken with the tenant's written agreement or not at all.
不得拍摄租客私人物品。

1.4 A tenant is not a party to the letting. Answer their questions about access
and timing; refer everything about the new tenancy to the landlord.

2. THE DIPLOMATIC CLAUSE 外派条款

2.1 The clause allows early termination where the tenant's employment ends or
relocates. It is negotiated before the agreement is drafted, not after.

2.2 Standard form: exercisable after 12 months, with 2 months' written notice,
and supporting evidence from the employer. A landlord accepting the clause is
accepting a real risk, so it is priced, not assumed.
外派条款通常为满12个月后、提前2个月书面通知。

2.3 A clause exercisable from month one is not a lease. Where a tenant needs
that flexibility, quote a shorter term instead and let the rent reflect it.

3. DEPOSITS 押金

3.1 The good faith deposit is held by the agency, not by the agent, and not by
the landlord, until the tenancy agreement is signed or the offer lapses.
诚意金由公司保管，不得由个人或业主持有。

3.2 If the landlord declines the offer, the deposit is returned in full within 3
working days. A returned deposit that takes three weeks costs a future letting.

3.3 The security deposit is a landlord matter and is held by the landlord. Agents
do not hold it, do not net commission from it, and do not advise on how much of
it should be withheld at the end of a tenancy.

4. REPAIRS AND REINSTATEMENT 维修与还原

4.1 The common threshold is that the tenant bears minor repairs up to S$150 per
item and the landlord bears the rest. Confirm the figure in the agreement; do
not assume it, because it is a negotiated number and not a rule.
小修门槛通常为每项S$150，须在合约中明确。

4.2 Fair wear and tear is not damage. A five-year-old wall that looks
five years old is not a deduction, and treating it as one is where most end-of-
tenancy disputes begin.

4.3 Reinstatement means the condition recorded at handover, not a new unit. The
inventory record is what settles this, which is why it is worth the hour it
takes at the start.

5. RENEWAL 续约

5.1 Open the renewal conversation 60 days before expiry. Both parties are making
decisions by then whether or not anyone has called them.
续约沟通应在到期前60天开始。

5.2 Bring the landlord a market position, not a number: what comparable units
have signed at, how long they took, and what an empty month costs against the
increase being contemplated.

5.3 Where the tenant has been reliable, say so plainly. A rent increase that
loses a good tenant and gains two vacant months is a worse outcome arithmetically
as well as commercially.

6. WHAT WE DO NOT DO 我们不做的事

6.1 We do not accept a holding payment without issuing a receipt the same day.
6.2 我们不代租客与业主之间传递未经书面确认的口头承诺。
We do not carry unwritten promises between landlord and tenant.
6.3 We do not sign an agreement on a client's behalf, whatever the urgency and
whatever the authority we are offered over the phone.
