# Helios Energy Systems — Field Handbook

## Overview

Helios Energy Systems manufactures the SunPak residential battery series.
The handbook covers installation, warranty, and support procedures for
certified technicians. Helios is headquartered in Boulder, Colorado.

## SunPak 5 Battery

The SunPak 5 stores 5.1 kWh of usable energy and weighs 48 kilograms.
Its continuous output rating is 3.8 kW, with a peak output of 5.7 kW for
up to 10 seconds. The unit is rated for outdoor installation between
minus 20 and 50 degrees Celsius.

The SunPak 5 ships with a 10-year warranty covering capacity retention
down to 70 percent of rated capacity. Warranty claims require the original
installation certificate, form HE-104.

## Installation Requirements

Installers must hold an active HX certification. Mounting requires four
M10 anchor bolts into concrete or a structural backing plate for wall
studs. The minimum clearance around the unit is 30 centimeters on all
sides for airflow.

Cable entry is from the bottom only. The maximum cable run from the
inverter is 15 meters; longer runs require the Helios Repeater Kit,
part number RK-220.

## Firmware Updates

The SunPak 5 runs HeliosOS. Updates are pushed over the air every
quarter, typically in January, April, July, and October. Technicians
can force an update from the service menu by entering code 4455 and
selecting "Check for updates." A forced update takes about 12 minutes.

Downgrading firmware is not supported and voids the warranty.

## Support Escalation

Tier 1 support answers within 4 business hours. Issues involving smoke,
burning smell, or visible battery swelling must be escalated immediately
to the Critical Response line at 1-800-555-0147 and reported on incident
form HE-900 within 24 hours.
