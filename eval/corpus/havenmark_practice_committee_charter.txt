Havenmark Practice Committee — Charter 实务委员会章程

This charter governs how the Committee is composed and how it works. What
counts as a finding, and how findings are graded, is set out in the Pathway; the
Committee applies those grades rather than defining them here.
本章程只规定委员会的组成与程序，违规分级以进阶体系文件为准。

1. COMPOSITION 组成

1.1 The Committee has five members: two team leaders, one Keystone agent, the
practice administrator, and one member from outside the transacting desks.

1.2 The outside member exists so that at least one person in the room carries no
transaction volume of their own. A committee made only of producers will
eventually find in favour of production.
委员会必须有一名不承担成交指标的成员。

1.3 Members serve two years. Terms are staggered so that no more than two seats
change in the same year.

2. QUORUM AND DECISION 法定人数与决议

2.1 Quorum is three members, one of whom must be the outside member.

2.2 Decisions are by simple majority. A tie stands as no finding, because the
Committee should not record against an agent on a split room.

2.3 The decision is final internally and is recorded in the case file with its
date, the members present, and the reasoning in no more than five lines.
决议记入案卷，注明日期、出席人员与理由。

3. RECUSAL 回避

3.1 A member recuses from any matter involving their own team, their own case,
or an agent they mentored through Ground.

3.2 Recusal is recorded. An unrecorded recusal looks identical to an absence,
and the two mean very different things when the file is read two years later.

3.3 If recusals take the room below quorum, the matter is heard by the remaining
members plus a substitute drawn from a Keystone agent on another desk.

4. TIMING 时限

4.1 A matter referred to the Committee is acknowledged the same working day.

4.2 Where a case has been suspended, the Committee convenes on the timeline the
Pathway sets for that grade of finding. Where nothing is suspended, the
Committee hears the matter at its next scheduled sitting, which is monthly.

4.3 An agent may respond in writing before the sitting, and is told the date at
least two working days beforehand.
顾问有权在会前提交书面说明。

5. APPEAL 申诉

5.1 An agent may appeal a finding once, within 10 working days, and only on one
of two grounds: that a material fact was not before the Committee, or that a
member who should have recused did not.

5.2 Disagreeing with the conclusion is not a ground of appeal. If it were, every
finding would be appealed and the process would stop meaning anything.
对结论不满不构成申诉理由。

5.3 An appeal is heard by three members who did not sit on the original matter.

6. PUBLICATION 公布

6.1 Findings are published internally, anonymised, once a quarter. The casebook
is written from these.

6.2 Names are not published internally or externally. The purpose is that the
next agent recognises the situation, not that this agent is recognised.
案例集不具名，目的在于让他人认出情境，而非认出当事人。

6.3 A pattern of the same finding across three or more agents is reported to the
partners as a system problem, not as three individual problems. Where a rule is
broken the same way repeatedly, the rule or the training is usually the fault.
