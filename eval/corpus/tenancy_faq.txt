Tenancy FAQ for Clients 租赁常见问题

Written for tenants and landlords, not for agents. Agents may send this to a
client directly. Where a specific document, deadline or fee is involved, the
transaction checklists are the authority and this FAQ does not repeat them.

本文面向客户，具体文件与时限以交易清单为准。

Q. What happens between my offer and getting the keys?

Your offer is recorded in writing, a good faith deposit is held, and the
tenancy agreement is prepared. Once both parties sign and stamp duty is
computed, the handover is scheduled. Your agent will walk the unit with you and
record its condition before you receive the keys.

Q. Why does the handover take so long?

Most of the elapsed time is not the paperwork. It is the landlord completing
agreed works, the previous tenant vacating, and utility accounts being
transferred. Your agent can tell you which of the three is the current holdup at
any point, and that question is more useful than asking for a date.

Q. What is the inventory list for?

It records the condition of the unit at handover, with photographs. It exists to
protect both sides at the END of the tenancy, when memories differ about whether
a mark on a wall was already there. Take it seriously at the start and it costs
you nothing later.

清点表是为了租约结束时保护双方。

Q. Can I ask for repairs after I move in?

Yes, but the position is much stronger for anything recorded in the inventory at
handover. Something first raised three months in is difficult to attribute.

Q. Who pays the agent?

For residential leases, the landlord pays the commission in the standard case.
Your agent will tell you if your situation is not the standard case, and they
are required to do so in writing before you make an offer.

Q. What happens if I need to leave early?

That depends entirely on what your tenancy agreement says, which is why it is
worth reading the diplomatic clause and the minimum term before you sign rather
than after. Your agent can explain what a clause means but cannot change what
it says.

Q. My agent says they represent both me and the landlord. Is that allowed?

Yes, with written disclosure signed by both parties before any offer is made. If
you have not signed such a disclosure and you believe both sides are being
represented, raise it — that is a serious matter and the firm treats it as one.

Q. Why will my agent not tell me what the unit is worth?

They will tell you what recent comparable transactions show, and where the
evidence is thin they will say so. What they will not do is give you a confident
figure they cannot support, because that number becomes your expectation within
a minute and everything afterwards is spent correcting it.

Q. What should I do if I am unhappy with my agent?

Put it in writing to the firm. A written complaint goes to the Practice
Committee within one working day. That is a defined process, not a courtesy.

书面投诉将在一个工作日内转交合规委员会。
