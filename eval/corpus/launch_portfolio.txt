Notes by project:

Marrow Gardens — Largest unit mix in the portfolio at four bedrooms and above. Two MRT stops from the interchange. The 480-unit count includes 32 dual-key units, which price approximately 4% above equivalent floor area.

Ellingham Rise — Freehold, small footprint. Only 214 units, no facilities deck beyond a lap pool and gym. Buyers seeking family facilities should be directed to Kestrel Bay instead.

Kestrel Bay — Waterfront facing for stacks 04 through 09 only. Stacks 01 to 03 face the service road and are priced at the lower end of the band for that reason. Do not describe the project as waterfront without naming the stack.

Portland Hollow — Highest indicative PSF in the portfolio. 128 units, freehold, city fringe. Expect a longer sales runway; this is not a volume project.

Wrenfield Park — Lowest indicative PSF and the largest unit count at 612. Earliest TOP in the portfolio at Q1 2027. Suited to buyers with a hard occupation deadline.

Salter Quay — 290 units, 99-year leasehold, TOP Q3 2028, the latest in the portfolio. Waterfront access is shared with two neighbouring developments and is not exclusive.
