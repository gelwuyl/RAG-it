SECTION 4 — LAND AND SUPPLY NOTE

Bayshore Road parcel closed at S$1,388 psf per plot ratio. Against Portland
Hollow's indicative S$3,120 psf entry, the land cost implies a launch price
materially above the current D09 portfolio band, and agents should not quote
Portland Hollow as comparable to a Bayshore launch.

SECTION 5 — CASE CLINIC (15 minutes)

Two cases were brought this month.

Case A — three viewings, no signed document. Escalated to Team Leader in the
same week, per Pathway Part E. Root cause was financing: in-principle approval
had never been sighted.

Case B — co-broke split disputed after an offer was made. The Practice Committee
found for the default fifty-fifty split, because the renegotiation was not
agreed in writing before the first viewing.

SECTION 6 — RULE TAUGHT BACK (10 minutes)

portal response standard, and why it exists — an enquiry answered after two
hours converts at roughly a third of the rate.
