# Sheet: NEW RESIDENTIAL
Stage | Rental Case Documents | Sales Case Documents
Before Viewing | - Viewing List (shortlist of units)
- Landlord unit info (floor plan, key collection form if needed) | - Viewing List (shortlist of units)
- Seller unit info (floor plan, recent caveats, key collection form if needed)
Offer Stage | - Letter of Intent (LOI)
- Good faith deposit receipt (one month rent) | - Option to Purchase (OTP)
- Option fee receipt (1% of purchase price)
Acceptance | - Tenancy Agreement (TA), two originals
- Stamp duty computation sheet | - Exercise of Option, signed within 21 days
- Sale and Purchase Agreement where applicable
Completion | - Handover Inventory List, signed by both parties
- Meter readings, photographed and dated
- Key handover acknowledgement | - Completion Account from conveyancing lawyer
- Keys released on confirmation of completion
- Final caveat lodged

# Sheet: COMMISSION
Case Type | Payable By | Standard Rate | Notes
Residential rental, lease 12 months or more | Landlord | Half month rent | Payable on TA signing
Residential rental, lease under 12 months | Landlord | Pro-rated, minimum S$800 | Approval required below minimum
Residential sale, seller represented | Seller | 2% of transacted price | Payable on completion
Residential sale, buyer represented | Buyer | 1% of transacted price | Waived where co-broke applies
Commercial lease | Landlord | One month rent per year of lease term | Capped at three months

# Sheet: TIMELINE
Milestone | Rental | Sale
Offer to acceptance | 3 working days | 21 calendar days (option period)
Acceptance to completion | 14 to 30 days | 8 to 12 weeks
Document retention after completion | 7 years | 7 years

# Sheet: DISCLOSURES
Trigger | Disclosure Required | Timing
Agent or immediate family has an interest in the unit | Written, to all parties | Before first viewing
Agent represents both sides | Written, signed by both | Before offer is made
Known defect affecting habitability | Written, to buyer or tenant | Before offer is made
Unit has been tenanted within the last 30 days | Verbal, minimum | At first viewing
Agent receives referral fee from a third party | Written, to the client | Before the referral is made

# Sheet: FINANCING CHECKPOINTS
Checkpoint | When | Owner | Blocking
In-principle approval sighted | Before OTP is issued | Buyer agent | Yes
Loan-to-value confirmed against property type | Before OTP is issued | Buyer agent | Yes
Cash-over-valuation gap acknowledged in writing | Before exercise | Buyer agent | Yes
Valuation report received | Before exercise | Conveyancing lawyer | Yes
CPF usage confirmed where applicable | Before completion | Buyer agent | No

# Sheet: HANDOVER INVENTORY

Appliances | Each appliance switched on and confirmed working | Yes
Keys and access | Unit keys, letterbox, access cards, remote controls | No, count recorded
Meters | Electricity, water, gas readings | Yes
