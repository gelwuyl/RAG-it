# Sheet: COMMERCIAL
Stage | Lease Case Documents | Sale Case Documents
Before Viewing | - Viewing List (shortlist of units)
- Landlord unit info (floor plate, fit-out condition, service charge schedule) | - Viewing List (shortlist of units)
- Vendor unit info (floor plate, tenancy schedule, outgoings statement)
Offer Stage | - Letter of Offer (LOO)
- Good faith deposit receipt (one month gross rent) | - Offer to Purchase
- Deposit receipt (5% of purchase price)
Acceptance | - Lease Agreement, three originals
- Stamp duty computation sheet
- Guarantor documents where required | - Sale and Purchase Agreement
- Vendor tenancy warranties
Completion | - Handover Condition Report, signed by both parties
- Fit-out deposit acknowledgement
- Access card and after-hours protocol issued | - Completion Account from conveyancing lawyer
- Apportionment of outgoings
- Tenancy novation letters issued to sitting tenants

# Sheet: COMMISSION
Case Type | Payable By | Standard Rate | Notes
Commercial lease, term 3 years or under | Landlord | One month gross rent per year of term | Capped at three months
Commercial lease, term over 3 years | Landlord | One month gross rent per year, first 3 years only | Balance by negotiation
Commercial sale, vendor represented | Vendor | 1.5% of transacted price | Payable on completion
Commercial sale, purchaser represented | Purchaser | 1% of transacted price | Waived where co-broke applies
Industrial lease | Landlord | One month gross rent per year of term | Capped at two months

# Sheet: TIMELINE
Milestone | Lease | Sale
Offer to acceptance | 5 working days | 28 calendar days
Acceptance to completion | 30 to 60 days | 10 to 16 weeks
Document retention after completion | 7 years | 7 years

# Sheet: DISCLOSURES
Trigger | Disclosure Required | Timing
Agent or immediate family has an interest in the unit | Written, to all parties | Before first viewing
Agent represents both sides | Written, signed by both | Before offer is made
Existing tenancy expiring within 6 months | Written, to purchaser | Before offer is made
Outstanding building or fire safety notice | Written, to all parties | Before offer is made
Service charge under review | Written, to tenant or purchaser | Before offer is made
