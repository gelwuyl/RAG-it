Havenmark District Guide 2026 区域指南

Purpose: a short read on each district Havenmark actively transacts in. This
guide describes CHARACTER, not price. Every figure an agent quotes to a client
comes from the current price list or a lodged caveat, never from this document.

本指南只描述区域特征，不作为报价依据。

DISTRICT 04 — Harbourfront and Sentosa Cove
Waterfront stock, mixed strata and landed. Buyer profile skews toward second
properties and foreign purchasers where eligibility permits. Rental demand is
seasonal and tied to expatriate placement cycles rather than to the school
calendar. Agents should expect longer marketing periods and should set that
expectation at the listing appointment rather than at week six.

DISTRICT 05 — Pasir Panjang, Clementi, West Coast
A mix of freehold boutique blocks and older leasehold estates. Freehold stock
here tends to be small — under 250 units is common — which means fewer
comparable transactions and wider valuation gaps. Buyers wanting family
facilities are usually better served in D15 or D23; saying so early builds more
trust than showing them three unsuitable units first.

DISTRICT 09 — Orchard and River Valley
The city-fringe prime market. Highest psf band Havenmark transacts in.
Transaction volume is thin relative to unit count, so a single closed deal moves
the visible average considerably. Do not quote a district average to a client
without saying how many transactions it is drawn from.

DISTRICT 15 — East Coast, Marine Parade
Consistently the firm's steadiest rental district. Waterfront-facing stock
commands a premium, but "waterfront" in marketing material frequently refers to
a development rather than to a specific stack. Agents must name the stack.

DISTRICT 18 — Tampines, Pasir Ris
Executive condominium territory and mass-market private stock. Buyer profile is
predominantly upgrading families with a hard occupation deadline, which makes
expected TOP the single most decision-relevant fact in a listing.

DISTRICT 19 — Serangoon, Hougang, Punggol
Largest unit counts and the widest unit mixes in the portfolio. Dual-key layouts
are common and price above equivalent floor area, which surprises buyers who
compare on total size alone. Explain the premium before showing the price.

DISTRICT 23 — Bukit Batok, Bukit Panjang, Choa Chu Kang
Lowest psf band in the private portfolio and the earliest TOP dates. Suits
buyers whose constraint is timing rather than budget ceiling. Shares district
lines with executive condominium stock of a different tenure class and a
different eligibility regime; the two are not alternatives and should not be
presented as such.

DISTRICT 25 and 27 — Woodlands, Sembawang, Yishun
Predominantly executive condominium and mass-market. Longest average commute
times of the districts Havenmark covers, which matters more to buyers than the
psf gap that motivated them to look here.

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Read it before a first meeting in an unfamiliar district, not during. A client
can tell the difference between someone who knows an area and someone who is
reciting a page, and the second is worse than admitting unfamiliarity.

不熟悉的区域，会前读，会中不读。
