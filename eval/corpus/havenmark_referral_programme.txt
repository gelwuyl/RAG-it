Havenmark Referral Partner Programme 恒安推荐伙伴计划

Who this covers: external introducers — mortgage brokers, renovation firms,
corporate mobility managers, and past clients who introduce more than twice a
year. It does not cover co-broke arrangements with other agencies, which are
governed by the Pathway.
本计划适用于外部推荐人，不适用于同行联合经纪。

1. PARTNER TIERS 伙伴等级

R1 — an introducer who has produced three or more transacted introductions in
the trailing twelve months. Reviewed annually. Receives the quarterly market
summary and a named contact at Havenmark.

R2 — one or two transacted introductions in the trailing twelve months.
Contacted at least once a quarter.

R3 — registered but not yet transacted. An R3 partner lapses from the register
after eighteen months with no introduction.
R3 伙伴十八个月无成交推荐即从名册除名。

2. FEE 推荐费

2.1 The referral fee is 15% of Havenmark's net fee on the transaction, paid
within 30 days of Havenmark receiving cleared funds. It is a share of our fee and
never an addition to what the client pays.
推荐费为恒安净佣金的15%，客户不另行付费。

2.2 No referral fee is payable where the introduced party was already a graded
client in the system on the date of introduction. The register decides this, not
memory.

2.3 The minimum payable referral fee is S$400. Below that the partner may elect
to bank the credit against a later introduction rather than take the payment.

2.4 推荐费不得以现金支付。
Referral fees are not paid in cash, and are not paid to an individual where the
introduction was made in the course of their employment.

3. DISCLOSURE 披露

3.1 Where Havenmark will pay or receive a referral fee, the client is told in
writing before the referral is made. This is the standard the transaction
checklists already apply, and familiarity with the partner does not waive it.

3.2 A partner may not describe themselves as a Havenmark agent, an associate or a
representative. They introduce; they do not advise.
推荐伙伴不得以恒安顾问名义对外沟通。

3.3 Where a partner is also a service provider to the same transaction — a broker
arranging the financing, a firm quoting the renovation — the two roles are
disclosed separately, because a client told about one usually assumes there is
only one.

4. SERVICE STANDARDS 服务标准

4.1 Each team maintains at least 6 active R1 or R2 partners. A team below that
number is depending on portal enquiries alone.

4.2 An introduction is acknowledged within one working day, and the partner is
told the outcome — including when the outcome is that nothing happened. Silence
after an introduction is the most common reason a partner stops making them.

4.3 每季度至少一次伙伴回访。
Partners are reviewed face to face at least once a quarter.

5. WHAT THE PROGRAMME DOES NOT DO

5.1 It does not create a co-broke claim. An introduction is not a viewing and is
not an offer.
5.2 It does not transfer client ownership. An introduced client is graded,
serviced and owned by the receiving Havenmark agent.
5.3 It does not apply to referrals between Havenmark agents. Those are an
internal handover, recorded in the case file, with no fee attached.
