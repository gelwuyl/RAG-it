Havenmark Valuation Notes 估价说明

How Havenmark reads a caveat, and what adjustments are defensible. This document
explains METHOD. It contains no prices and must never be used as a source for a
figure quoted to a client.

本文说明方法，不含价格数据。

1. WHAT A CAVEAT IS AND IS NOT

A lodged caveat records a transacted price and a date. It does not record the
condition of the unit, the motivation of either party, what furniture was
included, or whether the buyer was related to the seller. Two caveats at the
same psf can describe very different transactions.

契约记录价格与日期，不记录成交背景。

2. THE FOUR STANDARD ADJUSTMENTS

2.1 Floor level. Higher floors transact above lower floors within the same
stack. The gradient is not linear and flattens toward the top of a block. An
adjustment applied as a flat percentage per floor across twenty floors will be
wrong at both ends.

2.2 Facing and outlook. A protected outlook holds value; an outlook that is
merely current does not. Agents must check what is approved for construction on
adjacent land before describing a view as permanent.

2.3 Age and remaining lease. For leasehold stock, the decay is slow and then
sudden. Adjustments drawn from transactions in a different decade of the lease
term are not comparable, however close the psf looks.

2.4 Unit efficiency. Two units of identical strata area can differ substantially
in usable area. Dual-key layouts, long corridors and large planter areas all
reduce efficiency, and buyers comparing on quoted size alone will not see it.

3. WHAT IS NOT A DEFENSIBLE ADJUSTMENT

3.1 Renovation cost. What the seller spent is not what the market pays. A
renovation adjustment defensible at all is defensible only as a condition
premium, and a small one.

3.2 Seller expectation. Not a market input.

3.3 Asking prices of unsold units. An asking price is evidence of an ask, not of
a transaction. A comparable set built from asking prices will drift upward
indefinitely, because unsold stock is by definition priced above what the market
has paid.

未成交的叫价不构成可比数据。

4. HOW MANY COMPARABLES

Three transactions in the last ninety days within the same development is a
strong basis. One transaction is an anecdote. Where fewer than three exist,
widen to adjacent developments of the same tenure and age band before widening
the time window, because the market moves faster than buildings change.

5. WHEN TO DECLINE

An agent who cannot assemble a defensible comparable set says so. "I do not have
enough recent transactions to give you a reliable range yet" is a professional
answer and clients receive it far better than a confident number that moves
twice in a fortnight.

无法建立可比数据时，应当直说。
