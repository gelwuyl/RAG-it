Rewrite

Nobody asks a question the way a document answers it. Restated first, then searched.

Retrieve

Two searches at once: one for meaning, one for exact words. An invoice number and a paraphrase are both findable.

Rerank

A model re-orders every candidate against your actual question. Similarity is a guess. This is a judgement.

Deep searchgrep

Search ranks, and whatever falls below the cut may as well not exist. This doesn't rank. It reads every document word for word — if your words are there, they're used.

Judge

A second model checks the answer against its own sources. If it can't decide, it says so. It never guesses a verdict.

By the numbers

56 questions with known answers, over 27 documents. Some are unanswerable on purpose — refusing is a behaviour worth measuring. The corpus is confusable on purpose too: same firm, same vocabulary, different facts. The scored result ships with the app. Nobody has to run it.

Nine readings, three questions. RAGAS-style generation metrics — faithfulness, relevancy, correctness — plus the classic retrieval ones. Did the right passage come back, did it come back near the top, was the answer any good.
